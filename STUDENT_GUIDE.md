# Student Guide

Start with `/start-university`. Read the educational and security disclaimer, then select **CONTINUE CLASS**.

For each lesson:

1. Watch the professor video.
2. Review the objectives, topics, and supporting notes.
3. Select **TAKE QUIZ**.
4. Answer one A–D choice per question. Question and answer order are randomized.
5. Earn 80% or higher to unlock the next required step.

If you do not pass, the bot shows your score and topics to review, not an answer key. Review the lesson and ask for concept help in University Chat. Retakes follow the cooldown selected by administrators.

After every required lesson in a level, take its final exam. After every published level, take the comprehensive final. Graduation requires all lessons, level exams, enabled required assignments, and the final exam. A passing graduate receives a PDF Certificate of Completion.

Useful commands:

- `/progress` — dashboard and current step.
- `/continue` or `/current-lesson` — open the next step.
- `/my-scores` — latest attempts.
- `/help-university` — support benefits and University Chat.
- `/assignments` — enabled practical work and its status.
- `/submit-assignment` — submit text and an optional HTTPS file/image link.
- `/my-certificate` — retrieve an issued certificate.

The university is educational. It does not guarantee profit, certify a professional trader, or place trades. Never send anyone your password, seed phrase, private key, 2FA code, exchange login, or trading API key. The bot and legitimate staff do not need them.
