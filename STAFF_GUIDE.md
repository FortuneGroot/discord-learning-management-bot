# Professor and Mentor Guide

## Mentors

Mentors can use `/student` and `/student-progress` to see a student's current level, lesson completion, scores, and review needs. Mentors should explain concepts, suggest the relevant lesson, and direct students to University Chat or a support session. They cannot approve assignments, reset students, issue certificates, or create manual passes.

Membership support displayed by the bot:

- 4 × 15-minute one-on-one mentor sessions per month.
- 2 × 1-hour professor sessions per month.
- University Chat for educational questions and clarification.

Version 1 displays these benefits but does not schedule sessions.

## Professors

Professors have mentor views plus:

- `/pending-assignments` — up to 25 oldest submissions awaiting review.
- `/assignment-review submission_id approve feedback` — approve a submission.
- `/assignment-review submission_id reject feedback` — reject it with educational feedback.

An enabled, required assignment blocks the level exam until approved. Review attachment links cautiously. Never request a student to upload secrets, credentials, account statements containing private data, proof of a real-money trade, or an exchange login screen.

## Teaching standards

- Teach understanding; never distribute answer keys.
- Explain risk and uncertainty; never promise profit.
- Keep examples hypothetical unless compliance and leadership approve otherwise.
- Escalate account-security incidents to an administrator without asking for the secret itself.
- Do not say the certificate is an accredited degree, license, adviser credential, or proof of professional trading ability.
- Refer derivatives students to professor-led advanced instruction rather than encouraging unsupervised leveraged trading.
